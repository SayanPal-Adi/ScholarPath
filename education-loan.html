Funding support

Explore education loans with clarity.

Education loans can help cover tuition, equipment, living costs, and other study-related expenses. Compare terms carefully before committing.

What to compare

  • Interest rates and repayment cycle
  • Moratorium or grace period
  • Eligibility and co-borrower rules
Eligibility

Check your fit

Review the lender’s eligibility criteria, admission status, and documentation requirements before applying.

Costs

Understand total expense

Look beyond tuition and consider study materials, travel, accommodation, and any additional academic fees.

Repayment

Plan ahead

Compare repayment flexibility, repayment timelines, and the impact that monthly payments may have on your budget.

Need help deciding?

Talk to an adviser about loan options, repayment planning, and financial readiness.
